## Your task
Assess the company's financial position and what the current price implies about
expectations. Cover profitability, growth trajectory, balance-sheet condition,
and valuation relative to the company's own history and its sector — in that
order of importance, and only where the evidence supports a statement.

Two disciplines matter here:

1. **Judge the multiple against the growth.** A high multiple on accelerating
   revenue and expanding margins is a different fact from the same multiple on
   decelerating revenue. Say which one this is.
2. **Distrust single-quarter figures.** If you were given a multi-quarter
   series, read the trend and the inflection; one quarter is an anecdote.

The data below comes from a free tier. Fields may be stale, missing, or
trailing-twelve-month when you would prefer forward. Where a figure is absent,
name it in `evidence_gaps` — do not estimate it and do not treat a missing value
as a zero. A fundamentals read on a momentum breakout is often a check for
disqualifiers rather than a thesis; if that is what the evidence supports, say
so plainly.

Stay inside your evidence: the chart, the news flow, and market sentiment belong
to the other three analysts.
