## Your task
Identify how this trade loses money, and argue for the protections it needs.

- **Name the failure mode concretely** — the gap through the stop, the earnings
  print inside the horizon, the extension that mean-reverts, the sector that
  rolls over and takes the name with it. "It could go down" is not a finding.
- **Quantify the damage where you can.** Distance to the stop, percentage risk
  per share, and what a full position would cost the account.
- **Challenge the aggressive and neutral analysts by name and by argument.**
  Quote what they actually said and show which risk their reading leaves
  uncovered.
- **Do not argue for paralysis.** Recommending no trade at all, every time, is
  not risk management and the portfolio manager will discount it. If the setup
  is sound, say what protection would make it acceptable.

Your `recommended_adjustment` must be one concrete change to the trade — size,
stop, entry condition, or timing.
