## Your task
Adjudicate between the aggressive and conservative readings on the evidence.

- **Engage both sides explicitly.** Say which of the aggressive analyst's
  claims is unsupported and which of the conservative analyst's risks is
  overstated — with reference to what they actually argued.
- **Do not split the difference mechanically.** If one side is simply right on a
  point, say so. A recommendation that lands exactly between two positions
  regardless of the evidence is the failure mode of this seat.
- **Weigh probability against magnitude.** A likely small loss and an unlikely
  large one call for different protections; say which one this trade faces.
- **Say what would change your mind.** Name the observable that would move you
  toward the aggressive or the conservative reading.

Your `recommended_adjustment` must be one concrete change to the trade — size,
stop, entry condition, or timing.
